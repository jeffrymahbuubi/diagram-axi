## 1. Diagnosis (weak spots, evidence)

Round 3 (28 cases, 2 Sonnet reviewers each): both accept 23/28 (82%), per-reviewer mean 87.5%, either 26/28 (93%); checker 28/28 HARD-clean, 55 soft (report.json `summary`, `byCode`: arrow-crossings 28/28, track-spacing 14 in 7 cases, arrowheads-close 7 in 4).

1. **Flat dense view on excalidraw** (vitest-none, rejected by both). 25 nodes / 32 drawn edges, dagre DOWN, 4499x945 px, scale 0.69, `aspect-ratio` 4.8:1, 51 crossings (report.json cases[vitest-none]); labels on double-stroked horizontal bundles, 4 arrowheads stacked on `paths.ts`. No rule fired: `--group none` has no groups so `cross-group-edges` cannot fire; `denseReason` (src/extract/density.ts:62-67) needs > 1.5 edges/box on >= 15 boxes (here 32/22 = 1.45) or in-degree >= 7 (peak 4). The same graph on d2 (hono-none 33/19, self-none 38/24, fastify-none 49/24) was accepted. Root cause: the density measure ignores *width*: a flat DAG with a wide middle layer spreads dagre over ~12 columns. The problem is the excalidraw router's long horizontal runs, not the graph.
2. **Tiny, low-contrast d2 edge labels** (hono-default split; noted in most d2 cases). `src/engines/d2/translate.ts:65-66,194-195`: edge labels get `font-size: 13` and no `font-color`, so D2 draws them in its default grey; node labels get `#1e1e1e`. On a 2612x2690 scene scaled to 0.76 a 13 px grey `x3` on a crossing (`label-at-crossing`, hono-default edge-4) is unreadable. Also `track-spacing` 17 px over 1691 px (hono-default edge-26/28) and 12-13 px in fastify-none: `LANE_GAP = 12` in `src/engines/d2/lane-spread.ts:16` sits under the checker's 18 px `track-spacing` line, so ELK bundles stay bundles.
3. **Hub fan-out elbows** (hono-default): `middleware/` (uses 9, 7 shown) and `helper/ (used by 20)` sit at the top, their 7-8 capped out-edges span the whole height (`suppressHubs` keeps the 8 heaviest, src/extract/graph-ops.ts). Combined with 2 above it is the split verdict; by itself it is tolerable on d2.
4. **Excalidraw groups at the 12-14 box line** (self-max15 split): 14 boxes / 24 edges = 1.71, just under `EXTRACT_DENSE_RATIO` 1.75 for 12-14 boxes (density.ts:20-25); 60 crossings, labels on parallel verticals, the `lint/` title crossed by an arrow (not flagged: the excalidraw title box is inside the zone and the checker's `zone-title-crossed` shrink misses it by the PNG). Two ratio lines (1.75 / 1.5) are one knob too many.
5. **No entry mark on vitest** (vitest-max15 split, V-E1 fail in all 4 vitest views). `src/extract/entry.ts:32-38` reads only `bin`/`main`/`module`; vitest's package.json has no `main`, its `exports["."]` points to `./dist/index.js` and carries `__vitest_source__: ./src/public/index.ts`; `candidates()` (entry.ts:17-30) maps `dist/index.js` to `src/index.ts`, which does not exist (the source is `src/public/index.ts`). `bin` `vitest.mjs` imports `./dist/cli.js` (unscanned). Also: when the entry folds into a folder box (max15 collapses `public/`), nothing says where the entry went.
6. **Corpus-tuned workaround**: `src/extract/to-exd.ts:108-110` sorts edges by target because one order produced a ~62 px `arrow-through-node` HARD on d2/ELK in 1 of 28 cases. Root cause unknown; the D2 WASM takes only `layout|pad|sketch|themeID` (`src/engines/d2/wasm.ts:61-66,157`), ELK options are not exposed, and `spreadLanes` (lane-spread.ts:27 `THROUGH = 1.5`) only refuses moves that *create* intrusions, it never repairs one ELK made.
7. **Minor, recurring**: hub islands (10 cases, `observations.hubIslands`), crowded 3-line hub labels (21 cases), 7 d2 scenes >= 2500 px tall with PNG scale 0.63-0.76 (`tallPx`), stub `--` lines without heads (reviewers read them as broken arrows).

## 3. Evaluation design

- **Corpus A (tuning)**: the same 28 cases (`scripts/qa/extract.mjs`, shas pinned there). **Corpus B (held-out, pinned)**: 5 repos never opened during the fix round, 4 views each = 20 cases: `axios/axios` (`lib/`, ESM+CJS, flat dirs), `remix-run/react-router` (`packages/react-router/lib`, TS, deep), `vuejs/core` (`packages/runtime-core/src`, monorepo with workspace `@vue/*` imports), `nestjs/nest` (`packages/core`, TS decorators, barrels), `sveltejs/svelte` (`packages/svelte/src/compiler`, JS+JSDoc, deep). Pin: latest stable tag on 2026-10-25, peeled sha via `git ls-remote --tags`, recorded in the script as `HOLDOUT`. `--set core|holdout|all`.
- **Checker bars** (both corpora): 0 HARD on every case (today's `>= 90% HARD-clean` bar is raised to 100%), default views 0 HARD, the four `SILENT` lints silent, `arrow-through-node` 0 over the F7 permutation sweep (new script bar `through-node-sweep: 0/N`).
- **Vision bars**, 2 Sonnet reviewers per image, packet as today plus one line on stub lines: recommend **Bar V1 = mean of the two reviewers' V10-yes rates >= 90% on corpus A** (the honest estimator: with independent 90% reviewers "both accept" lands near 81% from noise alone, which is what rounds 2-3 showed); **V2 = default views 7/7 accepted by both**; **V3 = 0 cases rejected by both**; **V4 (generality) = corpus B mean >= 85% and 0 rejected by both**, scored once, after corpus A passes. A case rejected by one reviewer gets a third reviewer only for the note, never for the score.
- **Regression guards**: `npm test -- --maxWorkers=4` (d2 goldens regenerated once in U-H1 with `UPDATE_GOLDEN=1`, diff reviewed line by line: only `font-color/font-size` lines may change in `.d2`); `node scripts/qa.mjs --engine d2 --jobs 3` before and after U-H1 (HARD count and per-rule soft counts must not rise; record the baseline from main first); `test/golden/extract/*.exd` regenerated only by U-H2/U-H3 with the diff explained in the commit; `scripts/extract-eval.mjs` rerun (bar >= 12/15 per agent, unchanged).
- Reporting: `out/qa-extract-r4/` (A) and `out/qa-extract-holdout-r1/` (B): report.json, png/, vision-packet.md, verdicts-1.json, verdicts-2.json, `scripts/qa/vision-score.mjs` prints V1-V4 from the two verdict files.

## 5. Open questions (recommended first)

4. Edge labels: keep every `xN` (recommended; information) vs drop `x2`.
5. F5 auto `dir RIGHT` for deep d2 scenes: try and keep only if vision improves on both sets (recommended) vs never emit anything but DOWN.
6. Hub islands: leave and explain (recommended) vs draw the heaviest incoming arrow.

8. Reviewers: 2 per image (recommended) vs 3 with majority (more cost, less noise).
